What do these columns mean?

Every number is an expected value for the gameweek from FPL Copilot's model, which re-runs when bookmaker odds move or team news lands. A double gameweek adds both fixtures together; a blank gameweek is zero. Price and Rost% come from the official FPL site.

£ / Rost%
Current FPL price, and the share of FPL managers who roster the player.
Opp
Opponent. "vs" is a home game, "@" is away.
xMin
Expected minutes.
Start% / 60+%
Chance he starts, and chance he plays 60 minutes or more (needed for clean sheet points and the second appearance point).
xP
Expected FPL points. The nine columns to its right are where those points come from, and they add up to xP (give or take rounding).
xG / xA
Expected goals (penalties included) and expected assists.
CS%
His team's chance of keeping a clean sheet.

Points breakdown (expected points from each way to score):

App
Appearance: 1 point for playing, 1 more for 60+ minutes.
Gls / Ast
Goals and assists.
CS / GC
Clean sheet points, and the deduction for goals conceded (keepers and defenders).
Svs
Keeper saves (1 point per 3 saves).
DC
Defensive contributions: 2 points for reaching 10 (defenders) or 12 (midfielders and forwards) clearances, blocks, interceptions, tackles and recoveries.
Bon / Crd
Bonus points, and the deduction for yellow and red cards.

Gameweek shows one gameweek. Range adds the selected gameweeks together for each player; Per GW divides those totals by the number of gameweeks. Start%, 60+% and CS% are averaged over his fixtures.
